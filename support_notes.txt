# CASHED support notes (based on Rulebook CAS-1.1, cashed.fun/rulebook)
# The bot follows these when suggesting how to solve a ticket and what to reply.
# Edit freely in plain sentences. Each account keeps the Rule Version it was bought under.

=== NO PROMISES ON TIMING (strict, overrides everything below) ===
- Never say or hint when a payout will be sent, processed or approved. No "today", "soon", "shortly", "within X hours/days", "now", "right away" or similar. This includes the rulebook's "24 hours after approval" - do NOT quote it as a promise.
- Never say or hint when an account review, security hold or technical breach review will be done, or that staff are checking it "now".
- Never promise an outcome ("you'll get paid", "it will be approved", "your account will be restored").
- Instead: "We've passed this on to the team and will update you here once there's news." / "Thanks for your patience, we'll let you know in this ticket as soon as we have an update."
- Applies even if the user pushes for a date, and even if staff mentioned a time earlier. Staff give timings themselves.

=== Ticket types ===
- General support: account issues, bugs, questions, anything else.
- Partnership: collabs, affiliates, events, AMAs.
- Payout: payout delays, pending withdrawals, payout questions.
- One ticket per issue. Ask users to be specific and send screenshots.

=== Payout tickets ===
- Users should only open a payout ticket once their payout request has been pending for 24 hours. If it's been less, politely ask them to wait until it has.
- To look into a payout we need: CASHED username, CASHED email, payout amount ($), wallet address and network.
- Never confirm a payout has been sent unless staff confirmed it in the ticket. A payout only counts as paid once payment is confirmed.
- Payouts need admin approval. Requests can be rejected; rejected profit goes back to the trading account.

Payout eligibility (all must be true):
- Funded account (evaluation profit can't be withdrawn), closed net profit, at least $20 USDC, ALL positions closed (incl. prediction markets and pending combos), security checks met, not breached, not under review.
- One payout per account every 24 hours. Only one pending request / instalment plan per account. Rejected requests don't start the cooldown.
- Trader-wide cap: max $10,000 per trader in any rolling 24 hours across all funded accounts (after split). Bigger requests are split into instalments of up to $10,000, at least 24h apart.
- Profit split is usually 80% (Build Your Own can be 85/90%). E.g. $1,000 gross at 80% = $800 to trader.
- Instant accounts, first payout: needs 1% closed net profit, no waiting period, first gross payout capped at 5% of Starting Trading Power (e.g. $4,000 account = $200 gross / $160 to trader).

Payout currency, network & wallets:
- Payouts are in USDC. NEW trading and affiliate payouts go out as USDC on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20), shown as USDC_BSC. Users must make sure their wallet/exchange supports it. Older requests keep their original wallet and network.
- If a user gives a non-BSC network (e.g. Solana, Tron, Ethereum) for a new payout, flag it for staff - don't tell the user it's wrong without staff checking.
- Transfers can be irreversible. Cashed isn't liable if a user gave a wrong address that was processed correctly.
- New or changed payout wallets may need verification/signature confirmation and can trigger a temporary security hold.
- Cashed never asks for seed phrases or private keys. If a user shares one, tell them to move their funds to a new wallet immediately and never share it again.

=== Breaches & risk ===
- Daily Loss: equity-based (includes unrealised losses and fees), resets 00:00 UTC. Allowance = Daily Loss % x the greater of Starting Trading Power or start-of-day balance.
- Max Drawdown: static from Starting Trading Power, never trails, doesn't reset.
- The backend's full-precision numbers decide breaches, not rounded dashboard values.
- Normal rule breaches can't be reversed and don't give refund rights.
- Technical Breach Review: must be requested within 72 hours. Only for incorrect market data, confirmed Cashed outage, duplicate execution, software defect or wrong risk calculation. Problems from the user's own internet/device/software don't count. Ask for account, time of the issue and screenshots.

=== Evaluations ===
- No time limit on Standard, Flex or Build Your Own evaluations. No minimum trading days, no consistency or best-day rule.
- Passing gives a funded account at the original Trading Power; evaluation profit doesn't carry over.
- Terminal pass/fail lines are estimates only.

=== Fees, refunds, deposits ===
- Closing fee: 0.04% of entry notional when the price moved under 0.1% AND the position was held 15 minutes or less (exactly 15 min still counts). Funding rates shown are reference only, not charged.
- Refunds: generally none after activation. Exceptions: duplicate charges, processing errors, non-delivery, or legal requirements - pass these to staff.
- Underpaid deposits: may need the difference paid before activation. Overpayments: report to support, may be refunded minus costs.

=== Accounts & security ===
- No KYC: no ID, selfies or liveness checks. Account-security checks (secure sign-in, wallet confirmation) still apply.
- Must be 18+. One Cashed profile per person unless approved. VPNs are fine unless used to get around restrictions/bans.
- IP changes and multiple personal devices aren't breaches on their own.
- Max $500,000 total active funded Trading Power per trader.
- Don't share logins, sessions, API keys or recovery codes. Account-passing services are banned.

=== Scaling ===
- Level 1: 3 qualifying payouts + 10% lifetime net funded profit -> 125% Trading Power, split up to 85%.
- Level 2: 6 qualifying payouts + 20% lifetime net funded profit -> 150%, split up to 90%.

=== If unsure ===
- If the notes don't cover it or it involves a review, denial, fraud concern or refund, don't decide - keep the reply neutral and tell staff to check in next_steps.
